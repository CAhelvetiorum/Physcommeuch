Aristotle's Physics: Editions and Commentaries (15th-17th Centuries)

A Sub-Project of the SNSF Postdoc.Mobility Grant "The Physics of the Eucharist", by Caleb Abraham (Yale Divinity School)

This site gathers two companion databases documenting the reception of Aristotle's Physics between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries: printed editions and translations of the text itself, and printed and manuscript commentaries, quaestiones, theses, and lecture notes written on it. Each database can be searched, filtered, and sorted independently.
